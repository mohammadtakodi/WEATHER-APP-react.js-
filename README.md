## 🎯 What is ShadowMixer?

ShadowMixer is software that helps you stay private online. When you connect to websites or AI services, it mixes your data to break any direct link to your identity. This mixing stops tracking and spying. It works like Tor but focuses on protecting how AI systems see your requests.

The software uses a method called Fragmented Obfuscation. This means it breaks your requests into pieces. Each piece hides part of your data. By doing this, ShadowMixer turns large AI providers into "blind" ones. They do the work but never get to fully see who you are or what you want.

Key points about ShadowMixer:

- Protects your intent when using AI.
- Prevents linking of your identity to your actions.
- Uses multiple layers of privacy tools.
- Free and open source.
- Security based on zero-trust principles.
